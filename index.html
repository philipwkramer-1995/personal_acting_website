About Me

A dedicated actor bringing authenticity and depth to every role.

Based in New York City, I've been honing my craft through rigorous training at T. Schreiber Studio since 2019, studying Advanced Scene Study with Peter Jensen and Vocal Production with Page Clements. My background also includes improv training at the Magnet Theater.

My work spans film, web series, and stage, with recent lead roles in projects like Missing, Offboarding, and Orientation. I'm drawn to complex characters and stories that challenge audiences.
